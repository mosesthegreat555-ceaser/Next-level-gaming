OUR STORY

A PLACE TO PLAY TOGETHER.

Founded in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is a Johannesburg-based concept created around a passion for gaming and community.

Our goal is to bring together casual players, competitive gamers, schools and organisations through exciting gaming experiences.

OUR VISION

To be a leading destination for gamers of all levels.

OUR MISSION

To deliver memorable gaming experiences that unite players, schools and corporate groups.
